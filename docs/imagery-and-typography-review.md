# Imagery and typography review

Reviewed 2026-10-14 as an extension of the [proposed branding guide](branding-guide.md). This records the research baseline. A subsequent locally implemented icon/contrast correction is described below; fonts, hero artwork and accepted visual references remain unchanged.

## Direction

Keep the speaking portrait, geometric hero background and real workshop photography. They connect the blog to a person who teaches and builds systems. Orange/yellow already appear in the source favicon, hero, social preview and workshop material, strengthening the proposed palette. Keep Open Sans initially. The more concrete priorities are inconsistent icons, images containing small text and readable metadata, rather than replacing the whole visual identity.

The judgement that the angular background feels more dramatic than the reading pages is subjective. It can remain an identifying homepage motif. If a calmer variant is wanted later, compare the current background with one simpler treatment using identical copy and portrait positioning. Preserve the accepted composition until that comparison is reviewed.

## Image accessibility and theme behaviour

Use image purpose to choose alternatives. The hero portrait is currently decorative (`aria-hidden`, empty alt) alongside identifying copy; a background texture is also decorative. A linked avatar must contribute to a meaningful accessible link name. A diagram needs a useful explanation of its information, not just “diagram”. See the [W3C images tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/).

The training-cover Markdown currently says `![photo]`, which conveys little about its classroom context. Better descriptive wording is an editorial follow-up. A file-level alternative-text check does not guarantee that the description is useful.

Prefer live text for registration controls and testimonials where practical. This improves zoom, translation and theme behaviour and follows [WCAG Images of Text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/images-of-text.html), which has exceptions for essential presentation such as logos. Do not treat logos, historic screenshots and all text-containing imagery as interchangeable cases.

Keep the intentional article-diagram white canvas and the separately corrected workshop transparency. Do not apply global image inversion, dark overlays or white backgrounds. Diagram colours carry domain meaning: retain labels, legends and textual explanations under colour-vision simulation. Natural photographs do not need to match the palette or have a dark-mode version.

## Fonts: retain Open Sans, improve consistency first

The current family feels neutral and approachable alongside personal photography. That is an aesthetic assessment, not proof that it is the most readable font for everyone. A single family for navigation, prose and headings keeps the site coherent. A second decorative headline font would add a new identity and loading cost without resolving the demonstrated issues.

## Icon correction after owner review

The owner accepted retaining the existing orange/yellow geometric mark. The generator now produces consistent PNG and ICO output, including the default root favicon. Document/touch/manifest references use their real size-specific filenames; the inherited Apple app title now uses the configured name and the status-bar metadata uses the supported `default` value from [Apple’s web-app guidance](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AppleApplications/Reference/SafariWebContent/ConfiguringWebApplications/ConfiguringWebApplications.html). Unused HB source masters were removed; generated HB icons were replaced. The available mark is still a 60px raster, so large outputs do not gain new detail. Preserve its identity and consider a sharper equivalent master in a future focused refinement.

Generated-output tests reproduce the original missing-file failures and check the corrected images against their advertised sizes. CLI checks decode the ICO's embedded PNG. The research inventory above describes the pre-fix files; current results and deployment status live in [todo.md](../todo.md).
