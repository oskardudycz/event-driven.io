In an event-driven system, no single place shows which events exist, who publishes them and who reads them. A schema tells you an event's fields, not what it means for the business. This is a recorded hands-on webinar with David Boyne, who built EventCatalog, an open-source tool for documenting events and what they mean. You'll see how teams can make events easy to find and understand, so others can use them without guessing.
