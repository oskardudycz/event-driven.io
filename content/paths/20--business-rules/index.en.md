Teams spend a lot of time maintaining features nobody uses. The article starts with a real case: cloud full-text search over on-premises data that took about 90% of the cloud spending. When usage was finally measured, one customer used it, rarely.

Killer metrics are agreed with the business before you build. They define when you'll remove the feature, for example, if weekly returns don't improve by at least 5% within two months. You'll see how to reach such a metric by asking why the feature is needed, and what to check on your own when the business won't cooperate.
