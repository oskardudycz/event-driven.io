Welcome to the next week!

If you want to make God laugh, tell him about your plans. Everyone has a plan until they get punched in the face. We can and should make plans but also learn to let go.

We are no superhumans, or at least I am not.

Sometimes, we have a bad week at work, sometimes our daughter falls and ends up with three stitches in her head, and sometimes we wake up feeling terrible with the flu. Surprisingly, such cases always happen together, aye?

All of that has happened to me in the last few days, especially the nasty flu, which stopped me from delivering the proper Architecture Weekly this week. And also stopped from going to conference, and maybe made me postpone the workshop…

Still, not to leave you with nothing, I have prepared a [playlist with the 22 recordings of my conference talks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jnDchr5eabI&list=PLw-VZz_H4iiqUeEBDfGNendS0B3qIk-ps). You’ll find there a lot of materials around Event-Driven Architecture, CQRS, and Event Sourcing.
