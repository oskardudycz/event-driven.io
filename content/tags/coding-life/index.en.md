description: "Essays on the parts of a developer's work that aren't code: communication, meetings, risk, burnout and the habits that keep code simple."
---

Shorter, more personal pieces, mostly from 2021 to 2024. Some are about working with people: running meetings, communicating, toxic workplaces, workaholism and women in IT. Others are about habits in code: keeping it simple, being honest about risks, testing on production, storing dates. A few are updates on the Marten open-source project.
