description: "Three patterns explained with code: catching conflicting edits, coordinating processes across services, and reusing a function's result."
---

Three patterns, each in its own article with code. Optimistic concurrency detects when two people change the same data, so neither change is silently lost. Sagas and process managers coordinate a business process that spans several services, without a distributed transaction. Memoization runs a function once and reuses its result. The [Optimistic concurrency](/en/guides/optimistic-concurrency/) and [Distributed processes](/en/guides/distributed-processes/) guides go further on the first two.
