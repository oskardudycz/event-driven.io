description: "Event Sourcing stores what happened instead of overwriting state. You'll see how to design, store and change events, and when the pattern isn't worth it."

Event Sourcing records every business fact as an event and rebuilds the current state from them. You get the full history of your data, but also new questions: what to record, where to keep it, what to do when events need to change, and how to test it all. The articles below answer them, newest first.

If you're new to it, the [Event Sourcing foundations](/en/guides/event-sourcing-foundations/) guide puts the basics in a sensible order.
