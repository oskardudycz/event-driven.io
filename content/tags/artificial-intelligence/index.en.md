One article, written in 2021. It starts from a face-detection pilot that worked well only for white people, then goes through other failures: a credit card that gave a woman a much lower limit than her husband, and COVID diagnosis models that learned the wrong things. The point is that an algorithm is only as good as its data and the people training it.
