description: "Splitting a system into microservices solves some problems and creates others. Two articles on telling good reasons apart from hype, hiring and politics."
---

Two articles to help you judge whether splitting a system is worth it. The first goes through technical reasons and keeps the ones that hold up, such as parts of the system with very different load, or customers and regions that must not affect each other. The second looks at the human side: choosing microservices because they look good on a CV, help hiring or give a team its own territory. The [Monolith vs Microservices](/en/guides/monolith-vs-microservices/) guide goes further.
