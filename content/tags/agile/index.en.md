---
description: "Agile is about delivering what the customer needs, not about sprints and ceremonies. Essays on where common practices fall short and what helps instead."
---

Four essays on how teams work rather than on code. One argues that sprints don't make a team agile if the project never gets finished. One asks the business to bring problems instead of ready solutions. One shows how meetings and workshops drain introverts, and suggests written alternatives. The oldest, from 2015, is a personal story about perfectionism and side projects that never ship.
