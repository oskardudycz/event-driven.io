The largest tag on the blog, many of the articles being Architecture Weekly editions. Some are practical, like the outbox pattern, distributed locking, Kafka internals or PostgreSQL connection pooling. Others help you decide: how to slice a codebase, monolith or microservices, tech debt as a trade-off, and what to measure before building a feature. For a starting point, the [guides](/en/guides/) group many of these articles by topic.
