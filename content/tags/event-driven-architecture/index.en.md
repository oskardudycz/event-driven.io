description: "How event-driven systems behave once they run: messages processed late, twice or out of order, and read models that need rebuilding."
---

These articles look at event-driven systems in production. They cover how messages get processed and how a processor remembers where it stopped, what to do when events arrive out of order or more than once, and how to rebuild read models without stopping the system. Many of the recent ones use Emmett and PostgreSQL. For the basics, start with the [Event Driven Architecture](/en/guides/event-driven-architecture/) guide.
